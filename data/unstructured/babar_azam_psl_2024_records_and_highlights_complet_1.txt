PSL 2024: Babar Azam breaks Chris Gayle's record to become fastest to 10K runs in T20s   PSL 2024: Babar Azam made history after he became the fastest batter to score 10K runs in T20 cricket, going past Universe Boss Chris Gayle, who held the record for nearly 7 years. Courtesy: Peshawar Zalmi Sabyasachi Chowdhury UPDATED:   Feb 21, 2024 15:37 IST Babar Azam has become the fastest batter to score 10K runs in T20 cricket. He achieved the record on Wednesday, February 21, while playing for Peshawar Zalmi in their match against the Karachi Kings in the Pakistan Super League (PSL) 2024 at the Gaddafi Stadium in Lahore. Babar broke the record of Chris Gayle,  who achieved the feat back in his 285th innings  while playing for the Royal Challengers Bangalore (RCB) in their Indian Premier League (IPL) 2017 match against the defunct Gujarat Lions in Rajkot.

Babar Azam on a record-breaking spree When the Zalmi faced the Quetta Gladiators in their opening match of the tournament, Babar became the first batter in the history of the PSL to  reach the landmark of 3000 runs . He was looking good to go past the 10K-run mark against the Gladiators, but then Abrar Ahmed accounted for his wicket. Babar looked in brilliant form as he got to his half-century off 29 balls. He went on to score 68 runs off 42 balls with the help of 4 fours and as many sixes. The Lahore-born batter also put on a partnership of 91 runs for the opening wicket off 8.4 overs. However, his knock went in vain as the Zalmi lost the match by 16 runs. Babar has been a crucial member of the Pakistan team across all 3 formats. He was not in the best of form in the Test series against Australia, but he showed his class in the T20I series.
